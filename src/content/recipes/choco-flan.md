> [!info] ### 10-12 pax

#### Ingredients

**Cajeta / caramel layer**
- 250g cajeta (or dulce de leche)

**Chocolate cake**
- 140g plain flour
- 40g cocoa powder
- 5g baking powder
- 3g baking soda
- 2g salt
- 150g sugar
- 2 eggs
- 120ml neutral oil
- 180ml milk
- 5ml vanilla extract
- 120ml hot coffee

**Flan layer**
- 400g sweetened condensed milk
- 350ml evaporated milk
- 120g cream cheese, softened
- 4 eggs
- 5ml vanilla extract

---

#### Directions

1. Preheat oven to **175°C**

2. Grease a bundt tin well and spread cajeta evenly at the bottom

3. Make the cake batter:
   - Mix flour, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar
   - Whisk in eggs, oil, milk, vanilla, and coffee until smooth

4. Pour cake batter into the bundt tin over the cajeta

5. Make the flan:
   - Blend condensed milk, evaporated milk, cream cheese, eggs, and vanilla until completely smooth

6. Slowly pour the flan mixture over the cake batter
   - It will sink a bit. Do **not** stir

7. Cover tightly with foil

8. Place the bundt tin into a larger roasting tray and fill with hot water halfway up the sides (bain-marie)

9. Bake for **60-75 minutes**, until the cake is set and a skewer comes out mostly clean

10. Cool completely at room temp

11. Chill at least **4 hours**, ideally overnight

12. Run a knife around the edge and invert onto a plate

> [!NOTE] Notes of Importance
> - The "impossible" part is physics: cake rises, flan sinks, then flips when unmoulded
> - **Cajeta > caramel** if you can find it. Goat milk gives more depth
> - Coffee intensifies chocolate without making it taste like coffee
> - Overnight chilling dramatically improves slice integrity
> - A water bath is non-negotiable if you want clean flan texture
> - Don't use an overly decorative bundt tin; it increases sticking risk

### Banananana Variation

> - Optional variation: add **1 medium mashed ripe banana (100-120g)** to the cake batter and reduce milk by **60ml**
> - This makes the cake layer softer, denser, and more moist, with a deeper caramel-banana profile
> - Not traditional, but a strong variation if you want maximum eating quality over authenticity

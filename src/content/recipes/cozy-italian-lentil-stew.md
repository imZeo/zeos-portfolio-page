> [!info] Pax
> Recipe is for 4 pax

## Ingredients

#### Veggies

- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 bell peppers, diced small
- 2 medium potatoes, diced small
- Optional: 1-2 stalks celery or 1 medium carrot, diced small

#### Seasonings and Aromatics

- 1 tbsp coriander seeds, ground
- 1 tbsp fennel seeds, ground
- 3/4 tsp anise seeds, ground
- 1 tsp white peppercorns, ground
- Pinch dried chilli flakes, ground
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 cloves garlic, sliced
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

#### Stew Base

- 2 tbsp olive oil, for frying
- 45 g tomato paste, about 3 tbsp
- 200 g French green lentils, rinsed well, about 1 cup
- 400 g can crushed tomatoes, no salt added
- 1 litre water, add extra if needed
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp vegetable bouillon paste, instead of salt
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 handful fresh parsley, chopped

---

#### Directions

1. **Prep the vegetables**
   Dice the onion, bell peppers, potatoes, and any optional celery or carrot into small pieces.
2. **Make the spice-garlic-lemon paste**
   In a mortar and pestle, crush the coriander, fennel, anise, white peppercorns, and chilli flakes until coarse. Add the sliced garlic and lemon zest, then pound again into a fragrant paste.
3. **Fry the tomato paste and bloom the spices**
   Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the tomato paste and cook, stirring, until it darkens slightly and smells sweet and caramelised. Add the spice-garlic-lemon paste and fry for 30-60 seconds, stirring, until very fragrant. Do not let the garlic burn.
4. **Add and soften the vegetables**
   Add the onion, bell peppers, and any celery/carrot. Stir to coat in the tomato-spice mixture and cook for about 5-7 minutes, until the onions are soft and starting to turn translucent.
5. **Add lentils, potatoes, and liquids**
   Stir in the rinsed French green lentils and diced potatoes. Add the crushed tomatoes, 1 litre water, lemon juice, and all 4 bay leaves. Stir in the vegetable bouillon paste until dissolved.
6. **Simmer until tender**
   Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low. Simmer uncovered or partially covered for about 25-35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the lentils and potatoes are tender. If the stew thickens too quickly, add a splash of extra water as needed.
7. **Season and finish**
   Remove the bay leaves. Stir in the smoked paprika and chopped fresh parsley. Taste and adjust with more salt, black pepper, lemon juice, or chilli flakes as you like.

> [!NOTE] Notes
>
> - This tastes even better the next day; the flavours deepen as it sits.
> - If you want it soupier, add more water towards the end; for a thicker stew, simmer a bit longer.
> - Serve with crusty bread, over rice, or with a drizzle of good olive oil on top.

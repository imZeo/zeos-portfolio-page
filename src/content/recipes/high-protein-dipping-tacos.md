> [!info] 8-10 tacos, 4-5 servings

Time: 15 mins

### Ingredients

#### Seasoned Beef

- 450 g 93/7 ground beef
- Taco seasoning, to taste, about 1 packet
- Salt, to taste
- Beef broth, a splash

#### Spicy Dipping Broth

- 250 g beef broth, about 1 cup
- Hot sauce or chipotles in adobo, a spoonful to taste
- Smoked paprika, a sprinkle to taste
- Coriander seeds, a sprinkle to taste
- Lime juice, a squeeze to taste
- White onions, diced, a spoonful to taste
- Cilantro, minced, a spoonful to taste
- MSG or bouillon powder, a sprinkle to taste
- Salt, a sprinkle to taste

#### Tortilla and Cheese

- 8-10 corn tortillas
- 200 g colby jack cheese, grated, about 20 g per taco
- Cooking oil or spray, as needed

### Method

#### Step 1: Sear and Season the Beef

Preheat a griddle or pan over high heat until smoking. Add the ground beef and flatten into a thin layer to maximise surface area. Leave undisturbed for 3-4 minutes.

Once the beef has developed deep browning, scrape and flip it with a spatula. Season with about half of the taco seasoning or salt you think you need.

#### Step 2: Break Up the Beef and Dial In Seasoning

Using a spatula or potato masher, break up the beef into a crumbly, pebble-like texture as it continues to brown. Once the texture is where you want it, deglaze the pan with a splash of beef broth to add some moisture back into the meat.

Taste and adjust with more taco seasoning and salt until you are happy with the flavor. Turn off the heat and set aside.

#### Step 3: Make the Dipping Broth

Heat up the beef broth in the microwave or on the stove. Mix in all additional ingredients to taste, starting with small amounts and increasing until you are happy with the flavor.

- Dial the spiciness with the hot sauce, the aroma with the spices, and the taste balance with lime, salt, or MSG.

Garnish with diced onion and cilantro.

#### Step 4: Assemble Tacos and Crisp on a Griddle

Add a thin layer of oil to a hot griddle and lay down the corn tortillas. Onto each tortilla, spread about 20 g of grated cheese into a thin layer, allowing some to hang off the edge of the tortilla and get crispy.

Once the cheese starts to melt, add 50 g of seasoned beef to each tortilla before folding them in half. Continue until both sides are lightly crisped, the tortilla is pliable, and any melted cheese coming out from the edges begins to crisp.

#### Step 5: Serve

Transfer cooked tacos to a plate and serve with the warm dipping broth, extra onions, cilantro, or lime if desired.

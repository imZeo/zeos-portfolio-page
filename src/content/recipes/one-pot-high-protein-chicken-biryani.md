> [!NOTE]
> Recipe is for 4 pax

### Ingredients

- 600 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced
- 200 g basmati rice, well washed
- 35 ml ghee or vegetable oil (2 tbsp + 1 tsp)
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4 cardamom pods
- 4 cloves
- 2 red onions, thinly sliced
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 5 g ginger paste
- 5 g garlic paste
- 4 g coriander powder
- 3 g turmeric
- 2 g cayenne
- 3 g garam masala
- 1 g cumin seeds, or ground cumin
- 1 g black pepper
- Salt to taste
- 45 ml coconut milk
- 270 ml chicken broth
- To serve: cilantro, mint, lime, Greek yogurt

### Cookware Notes

- **Lightweight aluminium / Always Pan 2.0:** recipe works as written
- **Stainless steel:** increase oven time to 45-50 min
- **Cast iron / Dutch oven:** bring to a gentle simmer on the stove before baking, or raise oven temp to 180-190 °C
- **Ceramic / stoneware dish:** cover with foil, leaving a small gap for steam to escape

### Directions

1. **Soak rice**
   Rinse rice well, then soak in cold water for at least 20 min. Preheat oven to 160 °C.
2. **Bloom spices**
   Heat 1 tsp oil; fry bay leaves, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves for 5 seconds. Remove and set aside.
3. **Make the chicken masala, 10 min**
   Heat 2 tbsp oil; fry onions for 3 min. Add ginger and garlic; cook 1 min. Add tomatoes; cook 5 min. Mix dry spices with 1-2 tbsp water; add chicken and toss for 1-2 min. Do not cook through.
4. **Assemble**
   Add drained rice to the dish. Pour in coconut milk and broth; stir gently. Tuck in whole spices.
5. **Bake**
   Cover and bake at 160 °C for 35-45 min. Check at 35 min. If rice is not fully cooked, add 2-4 tbsp of water or broth, cover, and cook another 5-10 min.
6. **Rest, 10 min**
   Remove from oven and let sit for 10 min. Fluff gently with a fork.
7. **Serve**
   Garnish with cilantro, mint, and lime. Serve with Greek yogurt.

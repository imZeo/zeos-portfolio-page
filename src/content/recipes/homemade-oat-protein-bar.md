> [!info] Units
> Recipe is for 5 units

# Ingredients

## Protein Bar Base Layer

- 60 g oatmeal
- 75 g peanut butter powder
- 50 g whey protein powder
- 10 g dark cocoa powder
- Pinch of salt
- 120 g Greek yogurt
- 45 g almond milk

## Chocolate Layer

- 30 g chocolate
- 5 g coconut oil

---

#### Directions

1. Mix together.
2. Form and chill.

> [!NOTE] Notes of Importance
>
> Macros per bar:
> 199 calories | 19 g protein | 21 g carbs | 5 g fat
>
> **220 kcal | 19P | 18C | 9-10F**

## Ingredients

### Vegetables

- 280 g green cabbage, shredded
- 120 g onion, thinly sliced
- 80 g carrot, julienned
- 34 g ginger, minced
- 34 g garlic, minced
- 180 g bean sprouts
- 100 g scallions, cut into 2-3 cm pieces

### Noodles

- 900 g cooked lo mein noodles (about 450 g dry or fresh)
- Neutral oil, such as peanut or vegetable, as needed

### Sauce

**Dry ingredients**

- 38 g cornstarch
- 30 g sugar
- 2.4 g white pepper
- 6 g kosher salt

**Wet ingredients**

- 240 ml water
- 120 ml light soy sauce
- 120 ml oyster sauce
- 20 ml toasted sesame oil
- Dash of MSG

### Beef and Marinade

- 450 g flank or skirt steak, thinly sliced against the grain
- 1 g baking soda
- 3 g cornstarch
- 5 g sugar
- 1.5 g salt, a pinch
- 6 g light soy sauce
- 10 g Shaoxing wine
- 9 g neutral oil

### Final Touch

- 15 ml Shaoxing wine, for deglazing

---

## Instructions

1. **Marinate the beef**
   Rinse sliced beef under cold water for 1 minute, then drain well. Massage in baking soda for 1 minute. Add remaining marinade ingredients, mix thoroughly, and marinate for at least 15 minutes or overnight.
2. **Prepare the sauce**
   In a bowl, mix all dry sauce ingredients. Add water and whisk until smooth. Stir in remaining wet ingredients. Set aside and stir before use.
3. **Prepare noodles**
   Cook noodles according to package directions. Drain thoroughly and toss with a bit of oil to prevent sticking.
4. **Stir-fry vegetables**
   Heat wok over medium-high heat. Add a splash of oil. Stir-fry cabbage, onion, and carrot for about 1 minute. Remove and set aside.
5. **Cook the beef**
   Add more oil to the pan and heat until shimmering. Spread beef in a single layer and sear until browned. Remove and set aside.
6. **Build flavour**
   Add 1 tbsp oil along with ginger and garlic. Stir-fry for 10-20 seconds. Add noodles, toss with aromatics, and stir until noodles are hot and pliable.
7. **Combine**
   Return vegetables and beef to the pan and mix thoroughly with noodles. Move everything to one side of the wok. Add 15 ml Shaoxing wine to deglaze the empty side. Stir sauce to redistribute cornstarch and pour into the empty side of the pan. Let it bubble for 15-20 seconds to thicken slightly, then mix thoroughly with noodles.
8. **Finish**
   Add scallions and bean sprouts. Toss everything until evenly mixed. Serve immediately.

---

## Notes

- Make sure to stir the sauce right before adding, as cornstarch may settle.
- Prepping everything in advance is key to a smooth stir-fry process.
- Original recipe was waaay too salty, so water replaces chicken stock here.

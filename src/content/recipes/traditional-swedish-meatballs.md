Serve with mashed potatoes, lingonberry jam, dill pickled cucumber, and cream sauce for a classic Swedish feast.

---

## Ingredients

### Noisette (Meatballs)

- 1 1/2 dl milk
- 5 tbsp breadcrumbs
- 500 g ground beef or mixed mince
- 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp mustard
- Butter and cooking oil for frying

### Cream Sauce

- 5 dl cream
- 1 tbsp blackcurrant jelly
- Salt to taste
- Black pepper to taste

### Mashed Potatoes

- 1 kg potatoes
- 2 1/4 dl milk
- 50 g butter
- 3/4 tsp salt
- Nutmeg to taste

### Lingonberry Jam

- 3 dl frozen lingonberries
- 1 dl sugar

### Dill Pickled Cucumber

- 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1/2 dl vinegar
- 1 dl sugar
- 1 1/2 dl water
- Fresh dill

---

## Instructions

### Noisette (Meatballs)

1. Mix milk, eggs, breadcrumbs, salt, and black pepper in a bowl. Let it swell for 10 minutes.
2. Fry the onion in butter until softened, then add it to the mixture.
3. Add the minced meat and mix thoroughly.
4. Moisten hands with cold water and shape the mixture into meatballs.
5. Fry the meatballs in a mixture of cooking oil and butter, shaking the pan occasionally to keep them round. Fry for 5-6 minutes or until cooked through.

### Cream Sauce

1. Remove the meatballs from the frying pan.
2. Use the fat and juices from the pan, adding the cream and blackcurrant jelly.
3. Season with salt and pepper. Let the sauce simmer until it thickens.
4. Strain the sauce before serving, if desired.

### Mashed Potatoes

1. Peel and cut the potatoes into smaller pieces. Boil in salted water for 15-20 minutes or until soft.
2. Drain the water and mash the potatoes.
3. Heat the milk and add it along with butter to the potatoes.
4. Season with salt and nutmeg. Stir until smooth.

### Lingonberry Jam

1. Mix frozen lingonberries with sugar.
2. Stir every 10 minutes until the lingonberries are fully thawed and combined with the sugar.

### Dill Pickled Cucumber

1. Slice the cucumber thinly and place it in a jar.
2. Combine vinegar, sugar, and water, then pour over the cucumber slices.
3. Add fresh dill and let the cucumber soak in the fridge for at least 1 hour.

---

## Serving

Serve the meatballs with mashed potatoes, cream sauce, lingonberry jam, and dill pickled cucumber for a comforting, authentic Swedish meal. Smaklig måltid!
